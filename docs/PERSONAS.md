# 👥 User Personas

This document outlines the core user personas for The Pawn Shop application. AI assistants and developers must consider these personas when designing UI, planning features, and writing code.

## 1. Dale (The Regular)
* **Demographic:** 45, Local resident.
* **Verticals Used:** Primarily Pawn, occasionally Cannabis.
* **Tech-Savvy:** Low to Medium. Needs clear, high-contrast UI and straightforward navigation.
* **Goals:** Quick transactions, easy browsing of inventory, checking loan statuses.

## 2. Marie (The Compliance Officer / Manager)
* **Demographic:** 35, Detail-oriented.
* **Verticals Used:** All (Admin view).
* **Tech-Savvy:** High. Uses the platform for reporting and oversight.
* **Goals:** Ensuring age-gate logs are correct, managing inventory across verticals, generating compliance reports for PIPEDA.

## 3. Tanya (The First-Time Tourist)
* **Demographic:** 28, Visiting the area.
* **Verticals Used:** Cannabis, Fireworks.
* **Tech-Savvy:** High (Mobile primary).
* **Goals:** Exploring what's available, quick purchases, needs to understand legal limits and verify age seamlessly on her phone.
