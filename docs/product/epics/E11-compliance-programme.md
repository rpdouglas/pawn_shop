# Epic E11: Compliance Programme

**Milestone Gate (M7/M11):** Compliance hardened (M7). Legal jurisdiction sign-off received (M11 hard gate).

## 1. Executive Summary & Persona Focus

The overarching compliance programme covering PIPEDA data retention, indigenous jurisdiction disclosures, WCAG AA accessibility, and stolen goods blacklist management. The jurisdiction notices issue is a **hard gate for M11** — launch cannot proceed without legal and community sign-off.

**Primary Personas:** Marie (compliance manager — owns this epic), Makoonsii (accessibility is a first-class requirement for her trust relationship with the platform).
**Business Goal:** Operate legally within Akwesasne jurisdiction and Canadian federal law. Zero tolerance for age-gate or data retention failures.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria (Definition of Done)

- [ ] **PIPEDA data retention:** Monthly `purgeExpiredData` Cloud Function — auto-purge `pawnRequests` (no police hold) after 90 days, reseller leads after 1 year. `consentLogs` and `auditLogs` never auto-purged
- [ ] **Jurisdiction notices:** `/legal/` pages with jurisdiction disclosure copy for all three views. **Copy must be reviewed and approved by legal counsel before M11.** Hard gate — if delayed, launch date adjusts
- [ ] **Kanien'kéha phrases:** All phrases require community review and approval before publication. Documented gate in COMPLIANCE.md
- [ ] **Accessibility compliance:** `/accessibility` page. Full keyboard navigation audit on all three views. Tested with NVDA (Windows) and VoiceOver (Mac/iOS). axe-core in CI. All critical failures fixed
- [ ] **Stolen goods blacklist UI:** Admin UI to add/remove/search serial number blacklist entries. Integrated with E7 pawn form check

## 3. Schema & Rules Impact

No new collections. `purgeExpiredData` deletes from `pawnRequests` — requires admin SDK access (Cloud Function only, not client).

## 4. AI Implementation Guidelines

`purgeExpiredData` must verify `policeHold !== true` before deleting any document. Log each deletion to `auditLogs` with `action: "data_purge"`. Never delete `consentLogs` or `auditLogs` — add an explicit guard in the function.

Jurisdiction disclosure copy must not be generated by AI — it requires legal review. AI can draft a first pass for legal to review, but the final text must be human-approved.

## 5. QA & Runbook Considerations

- Runbook: if `purgeExpiredData` fails, documents are retained beyond their retention period — alert Marie; manual purge via admin SDK
- Accessibility audit: use both automated (axe-core) and manual testing — automated tools miss ~40% of accessibility issues
- Legal gate: schedule counsel review no later than S14 to avoid blocking M11
